drawing stress at every point, … and its shortfall grows monotonically with friction, from
1.7 % at μ = 0.025 to 26.7 % at μ = 0.1.»

**З2, стр. 9 — определения к замкнутому расчёту.** «where Δ is the inhomogeneity parameter
of the deformation zone and ⟨σ_f⟩ the plastic-work-weighted mean of the strain term … The
choice therefore does not carry the comparison.»

**З3, стр. 9 — замыкание по температуре.** «The temperature does not need a separate source,
because it closes on the deformation work itself: … ΔT = βσ_d/(ρc); since σ_d depends on the
flow stress and the flow stress on the temperature, this is a fixed point, reached in five
iterations. The friction on the land is left out of this balance deliberately: … is retained
because it is dissipated in the deforming material itself.»
